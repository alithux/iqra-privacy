# Iqra — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 September 2026**

Iqra is a prayer times, Quran, adhkar and qibla app for the Maldives.

Iqra has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. We do not collect,
store or share personal information about you. There is nothing for us to
sell, and no advertising in the app.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Iqra is saved on your own device and nowhere else:

- the island you pick, and your prayer time settings
- reminder and silent-mode settings
- your place in the Quran, your bookmarks and saved duas
- adhkar reading progress and your tasbih count
- your Home screen tile layout, theme and font choices
- the day's hadith, kept so the app works with the network off

None of this is uploaded. None of it is visible to us or to anyone else.
Uninstalling Iqra deletes all of it.

Prayer times are calculated on your device from a timetable built into the
app, so they work with no network connection at all.

## When Iqra uses the internet

Iqra works offline. It reaches out to the internet in only two places:

- **Quran recitation audio**, from `everyayah.com`, and only when you play
  an ayah.
- **The hadith on the Home screen**, from `ummahapi.com`, fetched once a
  day.

Like any web request, these servers can see the internet (IP) address your
request comes from, and may keep their own logs. Iqra sends them nothing
else — no name, no account, no device identifier, and nothing about what
you read or when you pray. We do not receive those logs.

## Location

Iqra does not collect your location.

On iPhone, the qibla compass asks for location permission because Apple
will not give an app compass heading readings without it. The heading is
used to draw the dial on screen as you turn the phone. Your location is
never saved and never leaves your phone. You can refuse the permission —
the rest of the app works normally.

On Android, the compass uses the phone's magnetometer, which needs no
location permission, so Iqra does not ask for one.

Prayer times come from the island you choose from a list, not from your
location.

## Other permissions

- **Notifications** — to show prayer reminders you have turned on.
- **Do Not Disturb access** — only if you turn on silent mode, so the app
  can silence the ringer during prayer and turn it back on afterwards.
- **Alarms and reminders** — to wake the phone at the right minute for a
  reminder. If you refuse this, reminders still arrive, just less precisely.
- **Start at boot** — to restore your pending reminders after a restart.

Each one is used only for what is described above, and only when you have
switched that feature on.

## Backing up your data

Settings → backup writes your saved settings and progress to a file and
hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely
your choice. Iqra does not upload it anywhere.

## Children

Iqra is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, children
included.
